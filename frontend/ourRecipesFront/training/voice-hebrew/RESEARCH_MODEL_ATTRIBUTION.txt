Changes: supervised Hebrew cooking action/intent classification and evidence
extraction. V10 research variants add same-report candidate ranking; V11
research variants add start/end evidence heads. V12/V13 investigate explicit
cooking state. The reference-language pilot adds a trained 200324-parameter
semantic head to a frozen, already adapted V12 control encoder; deterministic
code resolves state references. These are experimental adaptations and are
not endorsed by Dicta. Full encoder weights are kept locally outside the
material package; the reference package explicitly includes small semantic
head weights. Preserve this attribution when sharing adapted weights, and
follow the base model license terms. The small heads alone are not a model
that can understand Hebrew without the exact adapted encoder.

V5 research variants use an explicit abstention class (200453 parameters)
and positional pooling (400133 parameters). At the V5 stage, a separate rank8 query/value
adapter module for the last two BERT layers was prepared and mechanics-tested;
its Hebrew quality had not yet been trained or measured. Full encoder weights
are excluded; small research heads are explicitly included in the V5 packet.

V6 trains rank8 query/value adapters on the last two BERT layers while
retaining the adapted backbone unchanged, together with the400133-parameter
semantic head. The prepared adapter from V5 is now quality-trained in a
matched seed42 duration pilot; validation gates still fail. Small best/last
research checkpoints are included, full backbone excluded. No endorsement
by Dicta or production-readiness claim.
